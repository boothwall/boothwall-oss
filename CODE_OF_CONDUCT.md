# Code of conduct

BoothWall follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in the repo's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone speaks for the project.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, take feedback on the work rather than the person, and leave out harassment, insults and personal attacks.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, tell the maintainer, [@ddedic](https://github.com/ddedic), privately, through the same channel as security reports ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)): open the repo's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability** and start the title with "Code of conduct". Please don't raise it in a public issue. Reports stay confidential, and the maintainer acts on them as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
